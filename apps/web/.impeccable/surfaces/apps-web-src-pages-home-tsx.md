# Surface brief: storefront (web home, shop, order flow), shared with mobile and admin

Scope and mode: Persuade for the home page; the shop, product, cart, checkout and order screens are Operate-style task screens inside the same world. Admin is Operate and inherits the world's tokens only.

Audience, job, action: customers in the Philippines ordering celebration cakes, often on a phone, for a date and guest count. Action: shop a ready-made cake or design a custom one.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shop is a neighborhood panaderya's glass display case. Cakes sit on shelves, each with a clipped price card that says what it costs, whether it is there today, and how many days ahead to order. It refuses the stock bakery template (cream ground, serif display, centered hero over a row of equal cards).

OWN-WORLD: Committed rose (#9B5568 family) for the case back wall, awning and primary actions; glass-white and cool pale-grey for the room; a warm card-stock white only for price cards and order slips; steel-grey hairlines for shelf edges. Scalloped rose-and-white awning under the nav. Bricolage Grotesque for signs and headings, Hanken Grotesk for text, a handwritten face (Caveat) only for prices and lead times on cards. Corners are small (6 to 10px), like card stock. Availability is tape on the card: green today, amber pre-order with days written out, grey sold out.

STORY: The visitor sees fresh cakes on shelves, reads prices and availability at a glance, and can either take one home or start a custom design that the bakers approve before any payment.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Awning and nav at top. Under it a sign-painted headline "Fresh cakes, or design your own." with two actions, "Design Your Cake" (rose, solid) and "Shop Cakes" (outlined), set above a full-width display case: a rose back wall with two glass shelves holding four cakes, each with a clipped price card (name, From price, availability tape). The case is the hero; there is no side image.

FORM: The display case, position 1 of 7 on my list (panaderya glass case, Impeccable's pick; the roll's assignment was the piping-tip chart, number 6, and the user chose the pick). Seed key 550102b6.
